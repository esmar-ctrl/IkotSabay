About this project

The Ikot La Salle transportation tracker for De La Salle University – Dasmariñas.

About this project

The proposed transportation system aims to improve the overall accessibility, convenience, and efficiency of the Ikot La Salle transportation service within De La Salle University–Dasmariñas. It seeks to provide a more organized and user-friendly transportation experience for students and other members of the university community. Through the development of an accessible transportation platform, the system aims to enhance the way passengers interact with and utilize the campus transportation service.

Members: Jores, Sanchez, Ahmed

Methodology

Graph Theory is used in mapping the campus. To create a physical layout of the campus and jeep’s routes inside.

Algorithms is used in calculating the arrival time. Will be running in the background to convert live location updates into estimated arrival times (ETAs).
